# 🀄 Bangalore Club Mahjong Log — Member Guide

Welcome to the new Bangalore Club Mahjong scheduling portal! To ensure cooperative play and fair sharing of resources, please review this quick guide on how to book and manage your sessions.

---

### 1. Accessing the Portal
* **Website Link:** `https://bcmahjonglog.vercel.app` (bookmark this on your mobile phone or laptop)
* **Weekly Schedule:** The calendar runs **Monday to Saturday** (closed Sundays).
* **Rollover Time:** The schedule automatically rolls over to the next week every **Saturday evening at 8:00 PM**. Bookings for the upcoming week open at this time.

---

### 2. How to Book a Seat
1. **Choose Day & Slot:** At the top of the screen, select the **Day** and the **Session Slot**:
   * **Morning Session:** 10:00 AM – 1:30 PM
   * **Afternoon Session:** 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM
2. **Select an Empty Seat:** Scan the 9 tables in the room layout. Click `+ Book Seat` on any empty slot.
3. **Fill in Details:**
   * **Your Name:** Enter your full name.
   * **Add Guests (Optional):** Select if you are booking for yourself only, or adding `+1`, `+2`, or `+3` guests. The system will automatically reserve adjacent seats for your group.
   * **Start Time & Mahjong Type:** Choose when you will start playing and which style you are playing (Taiwanese, ATF, Singapore, Western, or Classic). 
   * **Enter Club ID:** Type in your Club ID (consisting of 1 letter and 3 digits, e.g. **A123**). *This acts as your password to cancel this booking later.*
   * **Reserve a Club Set:** Check the *"I need a Mahjong Set"* box if your group does not have a personal set. 

---

### 3. Cooperative Table Locking (Shared Rules)
To make sure everyone at a table is playing the same session:
* **First Booking Locks Rules:** The first person to book a seat at a table sets the **Start Time** and the **Mahjong Type** for that table.
* **Joining a Table:** Subsequent members booking empty seats at that same table will have their start time and game style locked automatically to match the first booking.
* **Club Sets Limit:** The club has **4 Mahjong Sets** available for use. If all 4 sets are claimed by other tables, the set-request checkbox will be disabled, and you must bring a personal set to play.

---

### 4. How to Cancel a Booking
If your plans change, **please cancel as early as possible** so other members can enjoy the tables.
1. Tap or click on your booked seat card.
2. Enter your **Club ID** (it is case-insensitive, so `a123` or `A123` will both work).
3. Click **Remove Booking**. If you booked with guests, this will automatically free up all seats reserved under your name.

---

### 💡 Friendly Reminders
* Bring your own Mahjong set if you have one, leaving the 4 Club Sets for groups who don't.
* Double-check your table's locked start time before completing a booking to ensure it matches your schedule.
